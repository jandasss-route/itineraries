[SPOTLIGHT · SOLIDARITY] SLIDE 03 / 10
STOCZNIA
GDAŃSKA
The shipyard where Solidarność was born in 1980. European Solidarity Centre (ECS) — permanent exhibition on the fall of communism. Raw industrial architecture, original crane structures still standing.
ECS · TUE–SUN 10:00–19:00 · ~€10

[LOCAL] SLIDE 04 / 10
FALOWIEC
PRZYMORZE
860 metres of unbroken prefab concrete, one of the longest residential blocks in Europe. ~6,000 people still live here. Nobody is visiting. That's the point. Nearby: head east to Nowy Port to see the 1894 brick lighthouse overlooking the industrial harbor.
OBROŃCÓW WYBRZEŻA · TRAM 8, 13
[NATIONAL PARK] SLIDE 05 / 10
WYDMA
ŁĄCKA
Moving sand dunes shifting up to 10m per year, swallowing entire forests. Unique landscape between Lake Łebsko and the Baltic Sea. Access on foot, bike or electric shuttle from Łeba. ~1h 20min drive from Gdynia.
SŁOWIŃSKI NP · ENTRY ~€4 · ŁEBA PARKING
[MEMORY] SLIDE 06 / 10
SZTUTOWO
STUTTHOF
First Nazi concentration camp established outside Germany (Sep 1939). ~2h visit — allow time. Afternoon detour to Mikoszewo: the Vistula meeting the Baltic, flat horizon, no crowds.
SZTUTOWO · FREE ENTRY · CHECK HOURS BEFORE
[LOCAL] SLIDE 07 / 10
HUNT FOR
AMBER
After Stutthof, head to the Baltic shore at Mikoszewo or Jantar. Walk the waterline and look for small pieces of amber mixed with seaweed, driftwood and fine debris washed ashore by the waves. The best finds often come after rough weather, when the sea has just rearranged the shoreline.
BALTIC COAST · LOOK LOW. WALK SLOW.
