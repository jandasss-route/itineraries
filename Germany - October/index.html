[GOTHIC · BAROQUE] SLIDE 03 / 09
ULM &
WIBLINGEN
Ulm Minster: the tallest church spire in the world at 161.5m (1890), rising over the Danube bend. Wiblingen Abbey Library (1744): two-storey Rococo hall with trompe-l'œil frescoes, carved columns and 15,000 volumes — one of the finest Baroque interiors in Germany.
WIBLINGEN · TUE–SUN 10:00–17:00 · ~€4
[KARST] SLIDE 04 / 09
BLAUTOPF
BLAUBEUREN
One of Germany's largest karst springs: a circular pool of intense turquoise-blue fed by an underwater cave system extending over 12 km into the Swabian Alb. The colour shifts with rainfall — deepest blue in dry summers.
BLAUBEUREN · FREE · 20 MIN FROM ULM
[CASTLE] SLIDE 05 / 09
SCHLOSS
SIGMARINGEN
Ancestral seat of the House of Hohenzollern, perched on a limestone cliff above the Danube. Current structure rebuilt after an 1893 fire — the interior holds one of Europe's largest private arms collections. Below the walls: the Kneipp Garten in the Prinzengarten for cold-water walking.
TOURS DAILY 09:00–16:30 · ~€14
[LOCAL] SLIDE 06 / 09
TWO
LOCAL RITUALS
DAY 1 — LEGAU
Aussichtsturm mit Hängebrücke — a wooden watchtower and suspension bridge over the Iller gorge. A Sunday outing spot for locals, not a tourist site. The bridge sways. The gorge drops. Free entry.
DAY 3 — SIGMARINGEN
Kneipp Garten in the Prinzengarten: stone basins of cold running water, barefoot paths and aromatic sections. Sebastian Kneipp's cold-water therapy — a German wellness tradition since 1850. Free, open access, castle walls rising behind you.
BOTH FREE · BOTH GENUINELY LOCAL
