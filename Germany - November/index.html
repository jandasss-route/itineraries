[DDR MONUMENT] SLIDE 03 / 09
KARL-MARX-
ALLEE
2km of Stalinist wedding-cake architecture — the DDR's showcase boulevard, built 1952–60 as a workers' triumphal axis. Café Sibylle at n.72: original GDR interior, small exhibition on the street's history. The mosaic on Haus des Lehrers depicts socialist life in ceramic tiles. Still all standing, still lived-in.
U5 STRAUSBERGER PLATZ · CAFÉ SIBYLLE FREE
foto3.jpg — STASI MUSEUM · MIELKE'S OFFICE · CAMPUS FÜR DEMOKRATIE
[SURVEILLANCE] SLIDE 04 / 09
STASI
MUSEUM
The former Ministry for State Security headquarters, preserved exactly as left in 1989. Erich Mielke's personal office — wood panelling, rotary phone, original furniture. 90,000 full-time employees who surveilled one in 63 East Germans. The campus is open to the public. The building still unsettles.
U5 MAGDALENENSTR. · MON–FRI 10–18 · ~€8
foto4.jpg — MÄUSEBUNKER · LICHTERFELDE · EXTERNAL FACADE
[LOCAL · EXAMINE] SLIDE 05 / 09
MÄUSE-
BUNKER
Built 1971–80 as a large-scale animal research facility — the name means "mouse bunker." Raw exposed concrete, angular ramps, ventilation towers like brutalist horns. One of the most extreme examples of German brutalism still standing. External visit only — walk the perimeter and photograph from the access road. Nobody else will be there.
S25 LICHTERFELDE WEST · 12 MIN WALK · FREE
foto5.jpg — TREPTOWER PARK SOVIET MEMORIAL OR MARZAHN PLATTENBAUTEN
[MEMORY · HOUSING] SLIDE 06 / 09
MARZAHN
& TREPTOW
Morning in Marzahn: Europe's largest Plattenbau estate — prefab WBS 70 slabs housing 170,000 people. The Museumswohnung is a perfectly preserved 1980s DDR apartment, original wallpaper and all — book ahead. Afternoon: Sowjetisches Ehrenmal in Treptower Park — the colossal Soviet war memorial with 7,000 soldiers buried beneath it.
U5 COTTBUSSER PLATZ · WBS 70 BOOK IN ADVANCE
foto6.jpg — GEDENKSTÄTTE BERLINER MAUER OR CORBUSIERHAUS FACADE
[WALL · BÉTON] SLIDE 07 / 09
MAUER &
CORBUSIER
Gedenkstätte Berliner Mauer (Bernauer Straße): the original death strip preserved — watchtower, border markers, documentation centre. Free, outdoor, devastating. Then west: Corbusierhaus near Olympiastadion — Le Corbusier's Unité d'Habitation in béton brut, 530 flats on 17 floors. Still residential. Final stop: U-Bhf Rathaus Spandau — the cathedral of the underground, brutalist in raw concrete.
