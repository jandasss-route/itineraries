foto2.jpg — MELNGALVJU NAMS OR RĪGAS CENTRĀLTIRGUS HANGAR
[OLD TOWN] SLIDE 03 / 09
VECRĪGA
& MARKET
Melngalvju nams: Gothic-Renaissance guildhall on Town Hall Square, rebuilt after WWII bombing. Trīs brāļi: the three oldest stone houses in Riga. Dinner at Rīgas Centrāltirgus — five repurposed Zeppelin hangars. Try rupjmaize, herrings and cumin cheese.
BUS 22 FROM RIX · ~30 MIN · ~€2
foto3.jpg — LĪGATNE BUNKER ENTRANCE OR SIGULDA CASTLE
[COLD WAR] SLIDE 04 / 09
LĪGATNE BUNKER &
SIGULDA Castle
Built in the 1980s for the Soviet Latvian party elite. Original furniture, red phones and maps still in place. Guided tour only — book ahead. Afternoon: Sigulda's Livonian Order Castle and Gūtmaņa ala, Latvia's largest cave with centuries of inscriptions carved into the sandstone.
BUNKER GUIDED TOUR · BOOK AHEAD · ~€12
foto4.jpg — RUNDĀLE PALACE · BAROQUE GARDENS
[BAROQUE] SLIDE 05 / 09
RUNDĀLE
PILS
The "Versailles of the Baltics" — designed by Bartolomeo Rastrelli (same architect as St Petersburg's Winter Palace) for the Duke of Courland, 1736. 76 rooms, Rococo state halls, formal French garden. Then Jelgava Palace on its island and wild horses roaming free along the Lielupe river bend.
RUNDĀLE · DAILY 10:00–18:00 · ~€10
foto5.jpg — ĶEMERI BOG · BOARDWALK · MORNING FOG
[NATIONAL PARK] SLIDE 06 / 09
ĶEMERI
BOG
Wooden boardwalks over a raised peat bog — mosses, dwarf pines and small teal lakes stretching flat to the horizon. Go before 09:00: October fog sits low and still. Ložmetējkalns: 27-metre wooden lookout tower with 360° views over the Ķemeri reserve forest from above the canopy.
ĶEMERI NP · FREE ENTRY · GO EARLY
foto6.jpg — JŪRMALA BEACH · SHORELINE SEAWEED LINE
[LOCAL] SLIDE 07 / 09
DZINTARS — AMBER
October storms push amber ashore. Search the dark seaweed line at the watermark. Real amber is warm to the touch and glows green-yellow under UV. Never cold like a stone.
PIRTS — LATVIAN SAUNA
60–70°C steam sauna with birch branches (slotiņa) for body massage, followed by a cold plunge. Latvia's answer to the Finnish sauna — available at Jūrmala spa centres.
JŪRMALA · MAJORI QUARTER · FREE BEACH
