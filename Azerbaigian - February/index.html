foto2.jpg — ICHERISHEHER · MAIDEN TOWER OR SHIRVANSHAH PALACE
[MEDIEVAL CITY] 03 / 09
ICHER-
ISHEHER
The walled inner city of Baku — a UNESCO World Heritage site dating to the 12th century. Maiden Tower: an 8-storey cylindrical fortress of uncertain origin, possibly pre-Islamic. Shirvanshah Palace: last residence of the Shirvanshah dynasty, built 1411–1501. Sandstone alleys, carpet sellers, tea houses still in use.
BAKU OLD CITY · UNESCO · PALACE ~€3
foto3.jpg — GOBUSTAN MUD VOLCANOES OR ROCK ART
[GOBUSTAN] 04 / 09
MUD
VOLCANOES
Azerbaijan holds more than half the world's mud volcanoes — 400 out of ~700 globally. The Gobustan field sits 65 km south of Baku: low grey cones bubbling cold mud and methane, a lunar landscape of cracked clay. Same area holds 6,000 petroglyphs carved between 5000 BCE and 1st century CE. Best accessed via local tour or taxi from Baku.
65 KM SOUTH OF BAKU · TAXI OR LOCAL TOUR
foto4.jpg — SHEKI KHAN'S PALACE OR CARAVANSERAI
[SILK ROAD] 05 / 09
SHEKI —
KHAN'S PALACE
Built 1762 without a single nail — the summer palace of the Khan of Sheki. Shebeke stained-glass windows: 5,000 individually cut coloured pieces held by wooden frames, no glue. Interior frescoes of hunting scenes and geometric patterns. The adjacent caravanserai (18th century) still functions as a hotel. Try the local piti stew and Sheki halva.
SHEKI · PALACE DAILY 10:00–18:00 · ~€4
foto5.jpg — LAKE NOHUR GABALA OR JUMA MOSQUE SHAMAKHI
[LOCAL · SEEK] 06 / 09
GABALA — LAGO NOHUR
Mountain lake at 1,560m surrounded by winter forest. In January the water is still, the air below zero, the Caucasus peaks visible on clear mornings.
SHAMAKHI — JUMA MOSQUE
One of the oldest mosques in the Caucasus, originally built 743 CE. Current structure 1860. Friday prayers still held — visit outside prayer times. Dress modestly.
BOTH FREE · MODEST DRESS AT MOSQUE
