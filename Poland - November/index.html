WYSPA
MŁYŃSKA
Mill Island on the Brda river. Historic 18th-century red brick mills built to connect the Vistula and Oder basins. Reflections on the water, cast-iron bridges, city park.
BYDGOSZCZ · FREE ENTRY
[NOC ŚWIĘTYCH] SLIDE 04 / 09
CMENTARZ
STAROFARNY
November 1st · sunset at 4:30 PM. Thousands of znicze — red and golden votives — lit across the graves between monumental trees. Silence, first frost, Polish cultural memory at its rawest.
CANDLES LIT FROM 16:30
[UNESCO] SLIDE 05 / 09
TORUŃ
GOTHIC
The only Polish city to survive WWII intact. Backsteingotik from the 14th century: walls, leaning towers, red brick churches. Birthplace of Copernicus (1473). Pierniki workshop — a living gingerbread tradition since the 14th century.

POZNAŃ
RYNEK
Renaissance Town Hall by Giovanni Battista di Quadro with arcaded loggia and clock tower. Tumski Island: 10th-century cathedral, baptism site of Mieszko I. Śródka quarter — timber-framed houses on the riverbank.
