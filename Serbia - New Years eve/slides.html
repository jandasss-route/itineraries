[BRUTALISM] SLIDE 03 / 09
NOVI
BEOGRAD
Built from scratch on marshland after WWII — the most ambitious Yugoslav urban project. Palata Srbije (1961) and Sava Centar anchor the axis. Genex Tower (1980): twin brutalist skyscrapers connected by a sky bridge. Blok 61 & 62 — monolithic residential slabs still fully inhabited.
JAN 1 · BUS 704, 706 FROM CENTRE
[EASTERN GATE] SLIDE 04 / 09
KAPIJA
BEOGRADA
Three brutalist towers marking the eastern entrance to the city along the highway. Built 1977–1980, nicknamed Rudo after the architect's hometown. Best seen at dusk when the facade catches the last light — or illuminated at night from the overpass.
FILOBUS 29 ~25 MIN FROM CENTRE
[NEW YEAR] SLIDE 05 / 09
SAVA
PROMENADA
Midnight at the foot of Kula Beograd — the 168m tower that defines Belgrade Waterfront. Free light show and fireworks over the Sava. No tickets, no barriers. Walk from Dorćol in 20 min. Dress in layers: Dec 31 averages ±5°C.
MIDNIGHT · WALK FROM DORĆOL
[ZEMUN] SLIDE 06 / 09
GARDOŠ
TOWER
Austro-Hungarian watchtower (1896) above the Zemun old town, a former independent city absorbed by Belgrade. Cobbled streets, Orthodox churches, fish restaurants (čarda) along the Danube. Feels like a different city — because it was. Jan 2 afternoon.
BUS 84 FROM ZELENI VENAC · ~30 MIN
